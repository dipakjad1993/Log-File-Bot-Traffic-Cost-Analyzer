GPTBot vs OAI-SearchBot: same vendor, opposite rules

GPTBot trains models — block it freely with zero live citation loss. OAI-SearchBot powers ChatGPT Search citations (crawl-to-referral ~85:1) — block it and citation share drops in 1–2 weeks. They share a vendor and nothing else: different user-agents, different IP lists, different robots directives, opposite edge policies. 5. Edge cases (2026)

  • OAI-AdsBot: allow-listed — it verifies ChatGPT shopping checkouts. Blocking it breaks revenue checks on ecommerce (the analyzer never blocks it, even if mis-tiered — tested).
  • ChatGPT-User: user-triggered, ignores robots ~54% — do NOT throttle (300/min abuse ceiling only). A 429 here means a missing live answer, not saved bandwidth.
  • GPTBot path-form variant (openai/gptbot) is classified training too — longest-pattern-first matching prevents shadowing.
  • Web Bot Auth: if your Cloudflare logs carry cf-web-bot-auth, the analyzer surfaces it as a strong positive alongside the IP JSON.

FAQ

Will blocking GPTBot hurt my ChatGPT citations? No. Training crawls don't produce citations; search-index does. The 1–2 week citation decay applies to OAI-SearchBot blocks, not GPTBot blocks.

Why 85:1 — is that guaranteed? No. It's a directional median from 2026 studies plus log measurement — ranges, not promises. Confirm against your own analytics before quoting savings.

What about Pay Per Crawl? Tab 6 generates per-path 402 pricing (free / premium / API tiers) with a tollbit-style worker — charge training bots per request instead of blocking, but never price search-index or user-fetch paths.
