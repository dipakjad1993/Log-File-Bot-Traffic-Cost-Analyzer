PerplexityBot: block or allow?

Allow — at 120/min. PerplexityBot is search-index with the best measured crawl-to-referral in the DB (~210:1 vs OAI 85:1, Claude ~5,143:1). Hard-blocking it trades pennies of bandwidth for lost AI-answer citations. Throttle training bots (GPTBot, CCBot, Bytespider) instead.

Copy-paste rule

# robots.txt
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
// Cloudflare: PerplexityBot 120/min/IP, 429 + Retry-After on excess
// Perplexity-User (user-triggered): do NOT throttle

How to confirm on your own log

Drop your log into the free analyzer (100% client-side, .gz + multi-file supported) → open Tab 5 AI Scraper Matrix → PerplexityBot shows cyan (search-index) with its 120/min policy. Verify via vendor JSON (https://docs.perplexity.ai/bot.json, snapshotted in data/bot-ips.json).
