1. What Anthropic actually publishes

  • Robots compliance — does the claimant respect the Disallow/Allow split below?
  • Forward-DNS confirm — does the hostname resolve back to the source IP?
  • Behavioral scoring — rate, breadth, and trap hits consistent with a real crawler?

3. Why Claude's ratio is the worst (and why you still allow search)

At ~5,143 fetches per referral, Claude-SearchBot is the most expensive citation in the DB — yet blocking it still costs Claude-answer visibility within weeks. The correct response is rate-limiting (120/min + 429), not blocking: you cap the cost while keeping the citation channel open. Training (ClaudeBot) has no referral channel at all, so it takes the full block.

4. Catch Claude spoofing without an IP list

  • Confirm spoofing server-side with reverse DNS before blocking — the tool exports an rDNS checklist (Tab 2 · Verification) with dig -x + forward-confirm commands.
  • Cross-check behavior: real ClaudeBot fetches broadly and respects robots; a "ClaudeBot" hammering /.git/HEAD or /vendor/phpunit is an exploit probe wearing a costume (Tab 9 · Security flags these).
  • Watch the UNVERIFIED share KPI — claimed-Claude traffic that never verifies is your spoof hunt shortlist.

FAQ

Will Anthropic publish IPs later? Unknown. The DB refreshes weekly by automation — if a list appears, verification upgrades automatically and the freshness pill shows it. Until then: robots-first.

Should I just block everything claiming to be Claude? No — you'd block Claude-SearchBot citations and Claude-User live answers along with the spoofs. Split by tier, verify by DNS, enforce per-policy.
