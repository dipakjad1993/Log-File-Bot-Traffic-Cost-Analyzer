The on-screen banner states sampling honestly — figures describe the analyzed sample and scale ~10× to the full file. For exact full-file totals (no sampling), node tools/cli.js --exact covers 500MB–50GB.

Why $0.25 — the blockable math

  • Blockable = training + suspicious only. Search-index (OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot) and user-triggered (ChatGPT-User et al.) are never counted — asserted in tests, so the CFO number can't be inflated by citation traffic.
  • Measured bytes × your CDN preset: CloudFront defaults here; switch to Cloudflare/Fastly/GCS presets in the pricing panel and every figure recomputes. Origin-compute stays OFF (logs can't tell SSR from static) unless you opt in with your own $/1K.
  • Cross-check: the 10k pinned fixture independently measures $0.04 total / ~$0.00 blockable ($0.0046 training-only) with byte-identical counts in CI.

Reproduce it

  1. npm run gen-logs -- --seed 42 --days 30 reproduces the 10k fixture byte-identically (see sample-data/EXPECTED.md).
  2. Drop any large log in the analyzer — watch Reading % (8MB slices), then the 14-step analysis progress (Web Worker past 20k rows).
  3. Compare your blockable share against $0.25/10×-scaled baselines in Tab 4, and your trap waste against 18% in Tab 3.
