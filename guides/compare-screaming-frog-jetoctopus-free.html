Where the paid tools genuinely win

  • Continuity: JetOctopus/Botify monitor daily with history and alerting; this tool analyzes point-in-time snapshots (Bot Dynamics compares two runs, but there's no scheduler).
  • Joined data: JetOctopus joins crawl × logs × GSC natively. Here the Crawl+GSC join is manual paste (one textarea each) — fine for audits, not for fleets.
  • Scale: past ~50GB, BigQuery/Splunk pipelines beat any browser. This tool's lane is triage + proof, with a CLI bridge (500MB–50GB exact) and a BQ pack beyond that.
  • Polish/support: a decade of Frog docs and JetOctopus onboarding vs a solo-maintainer MIT repo. Factor your team's time.

Where $0 wins anyway

  • Privacy: 100% client-side — no XHR/WebSocket/sendBeacon (auditable in METHOD.md). DPDP/RBI-sensitive logs never leave the machine. Every cloud competitor requires upload.
  • AI split depth: training vs search-index vs user-triggered enforced across 127 signatures with per-tier edge policy, 4-layer verification, spoof KPIs, and Pay Per Crawl v2 — purpose-built for the 2026 bot economy, not retrofitted.
  • Speed: drop file → GPTBot-vs-SearchBot cost split → copy-paste Cloudflare rule in ~30 seconds, $0 procurement.
  • Measured honesty: 1.02GB teardown with every number published, sampling banner on-screen, blockable asserted in tests (training + suspicious only — search/user never counted).

The honest bottom line

Paid tools win on continuity, history, and polish. This tool wins on privacy, speed, and price ($0). Start here free — run the 10k demo, check the 1GB proof, open the bot DB. Buy JetOctopus when you need daily history; buy Frog when you want desktop maturity.
